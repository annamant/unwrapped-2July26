# Unwrapped content system

## Goals (rank order)

1. Drive installs / visits → reserve a drop (`shopunwrapped.com`)
2. Build "local FOMO" habit — check what's dropping near you
3. Attract neighbourhood operators to apply (`/business-apply`) — shops, restaurants, freelancers, services, charities; anyone with in-person collection

## Content pillars (Instagram mix)

Aim ~ weekly mix:

| Pillar | % | Job | Example hooks |
|--------|---|-----|---------------|
| **Live drops** | 35% | Convert now | "Live near you." / "[VERIFY] ending in the next hour" |
| **How it works** | 15% | Reduce friction | "Reserve → QR → collect." |
| **Business spotlight** | 20% | Trust + local colour | Named local business + neighbourhood |
| **Culture / neighbourhood** | 15% | Brand feel | London mornings, neighbourhood trade love |
| **Business / supply** | 10% | Acquisition | "List a drop. Fill a quiet hour." |
| **Social proof / stats** | 5% | Credibility | Real counts only — mark `[VERIFY]` if unsure |

## Formats

| Feed portrait | 1080×1350 | Business spotlight, editorial |

| Reel (later) | 9:16 video | Process, neighbourhood energy |

Templates: `brand/svg/post-template-*.svg`, `story-template-ending-soon.svg`.

## Hook formulas (high engagement)

1. **Number + place + urgency** — "4 live drops · Hackney · before 2pm"
2. **Mechanism** — "Reserve in seconds. Collect with QR."
3. **Name-drop** — "[Business] just dropped. Limited."
4. **Contrast** — "Not delivery. Not endless stock. A real window."
5. **Instruction** — "Open the map. Pick one. Go."

First line of caption = hook. Soft CTA in last line. 3–8 hashtags max, niche-local over mega-tags.

## Caption skeleton

```
[HOOK — one line]

[1–2 lines of concrete detail]

[CTA → link in bio / shopunwrapped.com]

#london #independentshops #localdrops … (tight set)
```

## Cadence (starter — Instagram)

| Day | Default |
|-----|---------|
| Mon | How it works or culture |
| Tue | Live drops / map energy |
| Wed | Shop spotlight |
| Thu | Live drops or ending-soon story |
| Fri | Weekend preview / live drops |
| Sat | Story-heavy / UGC-style if available |
| Sun | Soft culture or business CTA |

Adjust volume to capacity; quality > daily spam. Minimum viable: **4 feed posts + 3–5 stories / week**.

## Engagement tactics

- Ask one specific question in caption comments (neighbourhood, favourite indie category)
- Reply to comments in-brand voice within 24h when human is available
- Stories: poll / quiz only when it serves discovery ("bakery or vinyl first?")
- Never buy engagement; never fake scarcity

## Approval gate

Posts leave the agent as `ready`. Human (or explicit "publish") moves them to `scheduled` / `posted`.

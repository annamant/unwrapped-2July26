## Required

### 1. Street
```
Street:             (must match STREET_BACKLOG.md name, or ask to add a new row)
Backlog #:          (e.g. 01)
Place:              (neighbourhood, borough)
Postcode area:      (optional, e.g. SE15)
Station:            (name + Tube / Overground / both — must be accurate)
BID name:           (if you know it; agent will still open the BID site)
```

### 2. Photos — one unique frame per slide if you can

The episode needs **six different pictures**. Crops of the same shot are a last resort.

| Ideal count | **6 unique** (one per beat) |
|-------------|-----------------------------|
| Minimum | **4** distinct photos — say which beats are missing |
| Long edge | ≥ 1500px |
| Crop | Croppable to **4:5** (1080×1350) |
| Quality | Clear focus · usable light · not grey mush · not heavy motion blur |
| Rights | **Ours to post:** Anna-shot / commissioned / licensed CC. **Council PR = look-dev until cleared.** No random Google grabs |

**Match the locked beats** (not “any three nice shopfronts”):

| Beat | Bring if you can |
|------|------------------|
| 1 Identity | Hero “this is the street” frame (shops OK as the opener) |
| 2 Scale | Depth / stretch looking **down the road** or a long parade — not a second shopfront portrait |
| 3 Then | Archive **or** the named historic landmark for that beat |
| 4 Now | People / life / vibe (or the place that stands for today’s energy) |
| 5 Twist | **The thing the curious fact is about** |
| 6 Ask | Soft civic / atmosphere closer |

Named landmarks you care about should have their own photo — they get a slide.

Optional filenames: `01-identity.jpg`, `02-scale.jpg`, `03-then.jpg` …

### 3. Handoff
- Chat: attach files + filled street block above  
- **or** folder: `marketing/instagram/drafts/YYYY-MM-DD-[street-slug]/your-photos/`
